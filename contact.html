Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our apps, sources, and policies.

Are PixelCraftin apps really free of ads and tracking?

Yes, absolutely. We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytic trackers, or background telemetry in any of our applications.

Where can I download the apps?

You can install verified releases on the Google Play Store or download direct APK releases from our official GitHub releases page.

How do I report a bug effectively?

Open an issue on GitHub with your device model, Android version, app version, and step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue.

Are the apps open-source?

Yes, all our codebases are open-source and hosted publicly on GitHub for community transparency, inspection, and contributions.
