01. Core Privacy Architecture

At PixelCraftin Studio, our applications are built around the philosophy that your data belongs entirely to you. Each application operates in a self-contained sandbox with strict privacy guarantees:

Fundamental Guarantees:

  • No Remote Telemetry: Offline utility apps do not request the Android INTERNET permission.
  • Zero Advertisements: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs or tracking libraries.
  • On-Device Processing: Calculations, documents, notes, and file operations execute locally on your hardware.
  • Minimal Permissions: We only request Android permissions strictly required for user-initiated actions.

02. Permissions Reference Guide

Below is an overview of standard Android permissions used across our catalog:

Permission Apps Purpose
VIBRATE Calc+ Provide tactile haptic feedback for keypresses.
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE PdfEditor Perform Image Compression, and PDF edits.
USE_BIOMETRIC Quik Note Unlock password-protected markdown notes via Android BiometricPrompt.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Quik Note Display background file operations progress and user-scheduled reminders.

03. User Controls & Data Deletion

You maintain full authority over permissions and data stored by PixelCraftin applications:

  • Permission Revocation: You can grant or revoke any permission at any time via Android Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Permissions.
  • Local Data Deletion: Uninstalling any application instantly and permanently erases its local database, preferences, and cache from your device storage.
  • Clear Storage: You may clear app data without uninstalling via Android Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage > Clear Storage.
