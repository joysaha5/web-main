APP PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy policy
for PdfEditor+

Effective July 23, 2026.
All app policies App details Source code
SUMMARY

Local data and
Android access

PdfEditor+ runs locally without account or network service. The app does not request Android's internet permission or send documents to PixelCraftin.

On-device data

PDF documents, pages, annotations, image compressions, and digital signatures processed locally

Android access

PdfEditor+ requests Storage / Media access strictly to read and save user-selected files.

Outside the device

PdfEditor+ does not upload documents or files to PixelCraftin or third-party servers.

Scope

This policy describes how PdfEditor+ handles information used by its features. PixelCraftin does not provide an account or cloud service for this app.

Data processed on the device

The app reads or creates the following information only as needed for its features:

  • PDF document contents, rendered pages, thumbnails, and user-added text or freehand drawings.
  • Digital signatures created with stylus or touch input embedded directly into output PDF files.
  • Compressed image assets and exported PDF copies stored in folders selected by the user.

Permissions and system services

Android access depends on the feature, Android version, and choices made in system settings. Relevant access includes:

  • android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_DOCUMENTS / Storage Access Framework (SAF) — To open, modify, and save PDF files and images chosen by the user.

Permissions can be reviewed or revoked in Android settings. Some features will stop working when their permission is unavailable.

Advertising, tracking, and data collection

PdfEditor+ does not request Android's internet permission. It contains no advertising and does not track users. The app does not send app data, document contents, usage records, or identifiers to PixelCraftin. It does not send data to third parties for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Other apps and services

PdfEditor+ does not send documents or files to PixelCraftin.

Android, app stores, backups and device-transfer services, and any external app or provider you choose to use operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and deletion

Edited documents remain in your designated storage folders. App preferences and cache remain on the device until cleared through Android settings or removed by uninstalling the app. Files managed through Android system providers follow those providers' storage, deletion, and backup settings.

Logs and support

The app does not send diagnostic or usage logs to PixelCraftin. If you report a problem, you decide what text, screenshots, recordings, or log files to share. Public issue reports are visible to anyone; contact PixelCraftin by email before sharing private information.

Security

The app runs within Android's application and permission model. All PDF editing and compression algorithms execute entirely on-device with zero cloud APIs. Its source code is public for review. Keep Android and the app up to date.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to pixelcraftinstudio@gmail.com.
