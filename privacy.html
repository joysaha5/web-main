01. Overview & Scope

Welcome to PixelCraftin Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of users ("you" or "player") who interact with our mobile software applications and video games distributed via the Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or playing any app or game published by PixelCraftin Studio, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms outlined herein, please do not install or use our products.

Key Principle: We design our applications and games to be lightweight and respectful. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we collect only the minimal data necessary to deliver smooth gameplay, core utility features, and stability improvements.

02. Information We Collect

Depending on the specific app or game you use, we may process different categories of information:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Customer Support Inquiries: If you contact us via email (e.g., at pixelcraftinstudio@gmail.com) for bug reports, feedback, or refund inquiries, we receive your email address and message contents.
  • User Settings & Preferences: In-app settings (such as sound toggle, theme selection, or control schemes) are stored locally on your device storage.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When our applications and games run, standard technical diagnostics may be collected automatically by Google Play or integrated libraries:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution
  • Operating system version (e.g., Android 12, 13, 14, 15)
  • App version and build number
  • Crash logs, stack traces, and performance diagnostics
  • Coarse non-precise geographic region (country/city level via IP)
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) — for ad-supported games, subject to your Android advertising preferences

03. Game Data, Progress & Cloud Saves

For games developed by PixelCraftin Studio:

  • Local Game Saves: By default, your level progress, unlocked items, high scores, and virtual inventory are stored securely on your device's private app storage.
  • Google Play Games Services: If a game supports Google Play Games Services, you may choose to log in with your Google Play Games account to sync achievements, post scores to global leaderboards, and enable cloud save backup across devices.

04. Android Device Permissions

Our applications and games only request runtime permissions when strictly necessary for specific features:

Permission Purpose & Usage
INTERNET Required for cloud saves, multiplayer, leaderboards, and crash reporting.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether your device has an active Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.
com.android.vending.BILLING Enables optional in-app purchases (e.g. ad removal, premium features) via Google Play.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback during gameplay actions or interactions.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional: sends game reminders or updates (can be toggled off anytime in Android settings).
WAKE_LOCK Prevents the screen from dimming during active gameplay sessions.

You can review and revoke any granted permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Permissions.

06. In-App Purchases & Billing

Certain apps or games may offer digital products or subscriptions (e.g. ad removal, extra themes, or pro tools).

All financial transactions are processed entirely through the Google Play In-App Billing System. PixelCraftin Studio never collects, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.

07. Advertising & Analytics Policy

In free versions of our games or utilities, we may display third-party advertisements to fund continuous development.

  • Non-Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized ads at any time on your Android device by visiting Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Ad-Free Option: Most of our titles offer a simple one-time purchase that permanently removes all third-party advertisements.

08. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

PixelCraftin Studio complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies.

  • Unless explicitly marked in the Google Play "Designed for Families" program, our apps and games are intended for general audiences (ages 13 and older).
  • For any titles targeting children, we disable behavioral advertising, do not collect personal identifiers, and only use certified family-safe ad networks.
  • If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at pixelcraftinstudio@gmail.com for immediate deletion.
